Release Proof

Glide Roman and Glide Italic, side by side.

This page targets the generated release in build/release/fonts. Roman comes from extracted checkpoints of src/glide-variable.ttf; Italic is the seeded branch rebuilt after removing the fragile direct-delta transfer for the worst cap, symbol, and diagonal glyphs. The preview uses real CSS font-weight and font-style matching, with synthesis disabled, so you are looking at the packaged variable-font outputs rather than faux oblique or low-level axis plumbing.
